## How I approach AI transformation

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Business problem] --> B[Process mapping]
    B --> C[Data & integration design]
    C --> D[Automation / AI architecture]
    D --> E[Build & integrate]
    E --> F[Test & verify]
    F --> G[Measure business impact]
    G --> H[Iterate / scale]
```

I start with the **business process**, not the model. AI is introduced only where it improves a measurable part of the workflow.

---

## Architecture principles

1. **Business-first** — solve an operational problem before adding AI.
2. **Human control where it matters** — approvals and escalation for sensitive actions.
3. **Structured data before automation** — reliable workflows need reliable state.
4. **API-first integrations** — prefer maintainable interfaces over brittle manual steps.
5. **Verification over assumption** — execution and outputs should be inspectable.
6. **Cost-aware model selection** — use the model/infrastructure appropriate to the task.
7. **Privacy & governance by design** — local/private deployment when business or regulatory requirements justify it.
8. **Do not over-engineer** — choose SaaS, API, open-source or local AI according to the business requirement.
